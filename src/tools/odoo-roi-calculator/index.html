How this is calculated

Benefits at full run-rate (per year):

  • Labour = staff × monthly cost × 12 × time recovered
  • Inventory = inventory × reduction × (bank rate + carrying cost)
  • Procurement = annual spend × savings rate
  • Production = production value × (waste today − waste after)
  • Finance = (receivables ÷ DSO) × days saved × bank rate

The ramp. Nothing is counted in hypercare (months 1–3). Labour and procurement benefits start in month 4, inventory and collections in month 7, production and waste in month 10. Each reaches full run-rate over 3–4 months. That's why the ROI here is lower than the simple formula, and more honest.

Costs. One-time costs are counted at go-live. Recurring costs are spread evenly across 36 months.

ROI = (3-year benefits − 3-year cost) ÷ 3-year cost. For comparison, the simple payback in article 0048 divides the 3-year cost by the full monthly benefit, with no ramp. It's shown in the note above.

Use it for direction, not decisions alone. The benchmarks come from Bangladesh manufacturing projects and represent the middle of the range. Your result depends on data quality, adoption and scope discipline. The readiness assessment tells you how likely you are to actually get these benefits.
