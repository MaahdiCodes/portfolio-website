Where these numbers come from. They're the same model the cost estimator uses: implementation bands from real Bangladesh projects, Odoo's published Bangladesh-tier licence prices, and the line-item ranges in Hidden ERP costs and the 2026 cost guide. They're practitioner benchmarks, not survey results. A market survey is planned, and when it runs this page will be updated with its data.

2. Industry adjusts the range

Complexity isn't the same across industries. Multiply the band above by roughly:

  • Services / retail: × 0.95. Fewer stock and costing edge cases.
  • Trading / distribution: × 1.0, the baseline.
  • General manufacturing: × 1.15. BOMs, work centres and costing.
  • Textile: × 1.25. Lots, shades, process losses and bonded stock.
  • RMG / garments: × 1.3. Style/size/colour variants, subcontracting, wage-board payroll, LC.
  • Cycle / light-engineering export: × 1.4. Deep BOMs, export documentation and duty drawback.
  • Pharma: × 1.5. Batch traceability, quality control and regulatory records.

4. The Odoo licence (paid to Odoo S.A., not the partner)

Bangladesh-tier Enterprise pricing, per user per month on yearly billing: Standard $7.25 in year 1, then $8.95. Custom $10.90 in year 1, then $13.60. Custom is needed for Studio, multi-company, external API and Odoo.sh. The year-1 price is a promotion that applies only to users ordered upfront. Renewals can rise by up to 7% a year, and monthly billing costs about 22% more. The licence should be in your company's name and shown separately from the partner's fee. See the licensing deep-dive.

5. Consultant rates

Qualified local Odoo consultants charge roughly ৳1,500–4,000 per hour. Internationally positioned firms charge $50–120 per hour. The rate matters less than the hours estimated, so compare the scope and the hours, not the rate card.

6. Price points that should worry you

  • Too cheap: ৳5–8 lakh for a full manufacturing implementation almost always means data migration, UAT, training or hypercare has been left out. A quote of 6–8 weeks for a multi-department manufacturer is configuration time only.
  • Too expensive: ৳40 lakh or more for a 50-person company on standard modules deserves a line-by-line breakdown.
  • Vague: "customisation as required", "training included" with no hours, or "support" with no SLA. These aren't prices. They're future invoices.
  • Hidden licence mark-up: an Odoo licence bundled into the partner fee at more than Odoo's published price, or held in the partner's name.

7. Budget beyond the quote

Even a complete quote isn't the whole cost. Your own team's time (typically ৳5–15 lakh of opportunity cost), hardware and a backup internet link (৳2–8 lakh), refresher training as staff turn over (৳1–2 lakh a year) and annual support (৳1.5–6 lakh a year) sit outside it. Add a 15–25% contingency. The ROI calculator shows whether it all still pays back.
