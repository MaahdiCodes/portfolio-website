The gap between cash at port and landed cost is working capital, not cost. If your costing sheet adds VAT or AIT to product cost, your prices are too high. In Odoo, only CD, RD, SD and local charges belong in the landed-cost lines.

Formula and sources
  • Assessable value = CIF + landing charge of 1% of CIF (Bangladesh Customs).
  • CD and RD are charged on AV. SD is charged on AV + CD + RD.
  • VAT and AT are charged on AV + CD + RD + SD. AIT is charged on AV.
  • TTI (total tax incidence) = all import taxes ÷ AV. It's the figure traders quote, and it overstates cost for VAT-registered importers.

Rates change every June with the budget and are set per HS code, sometimes with SRO exemptions. The FY 2026-27 Finance Act changed several (for example, AT on POS machines and EV chargers went from 7.5% to 0%, and some cosmetics' SD was cut). Look up your HS code on the Bangladesh Customs operative tariff and check Compliance Watch for recent changes.

Estimate only. The assessed value, exchange rate and applicable SROs on your bill of entry decide what you actually pay. Use this for costing and cash planning, and confirm with your C&F agent.
