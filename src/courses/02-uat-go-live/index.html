Why this course exists

Most ERP failures aren't configuration failures. They happen in the last 20% of the project. UAT becomes a formality, opening stock comes from the old system instead of a count, training is a demo, and nobody agreed what "ready" meant. This course is a practical playbook for that last stretch. Each lesson ends with an exercise you can do on your own project, and links to a free template.

What you'll be able to do

  • Define yes/no readiness criteria and plan around the Bangladesh calendar
  • Write UAT scripts that catch the edge cases, including VAT, TDS, VDS, landed cost and payroll
  • Run UAT cycles with one defect log and honest severity
  • Cut over data and reconcile it to the taka
  • Train by role and assess practically
  • Run the go/no-go meeting and a rehearsed cut-over weekend
  • Run hypercare, the first month-end close, and exit to BAU
