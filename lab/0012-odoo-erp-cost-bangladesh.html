The 5 Cost Components of Any Odoo ERP Project

Odoo ERP implementation cost in Bangladesh is made up of five distinct buckets. Misunderstanding any one of them leads to budget overruns.

  1. Odoo License Cost — Community (free) vs Enterprise (paid per user)
  2. Consulting & Implementation Fee — Discovery, configuration, customization
  3. Data Migration — Cleaning and importing your existing data
  4. Training — End-user and administrator training
  5. Hosting & Ongoing Support — Server, maintenance, future upgrades

What Drives Cost Up (And How to Control It)

Customization is the biggest cost inflator. Every time a business says "Odoo doesn't work exactly the way we do it," that's a customization request. My strong advice: in Phase 1, adapt your process to Odoo's standard workflow. Customize only what genuinely has no standard equivalent.

Data quality is the second-largest driver. If your existing data (inventory, customers, bills of material) is scattered across spreadsheets with no clean master data, expect 30–50% of your migration budget to go into data cleaning — before a single record is imported into Odoo.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Odoo Community free in Bangladesh?

Yes. Odoo Community is free and open-source. You pay for implementation, hosting, and support — but not the software itself. The trade-off is losing access to Enterprise-only modules like advanced manufacturing, studio, and mobile apps.

What is the cheapest way to implement Odoo in Bangladesh?

Start with Odoo Community, limit your first phase to 3 core modules (Inventory, Sales, Purchase), use a local Odoo consultant instead of an international firm, and resist customizations in Phase 1. A disciplined SME starter can go live for under BDT 3 lakh.

How long does Odoo ERP implementation take in Bangladesh?

SME projects typically take 6–10 weeks from kick-off to go-live. Multi-site enterprise implementations like a national manufacturing rollout take 6–18 months depending on complexity, number of sites, and organizational change management requirements.
