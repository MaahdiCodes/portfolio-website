How much does Odoo ERP implementation cost in Bangladesh?

+

Odoo ERP implementation cost in Bangladesh typically ranges from BDT 2–5 lakh for SME deployments (3–5 core modules, up to 25 users) to BDT 10–30+ lakh for enterprise multi-site projects. Cost depends on: number of Odoo modules, customization needs, data migration complexity, and user count. Contact Mehedi for a scoped estimate.

What industries does Odoo ERP support in Bangladesh?

+

Odoo ERP is widely deployed across manufacturing, RMG (Ready Made Garments), textiles, cycle export, FMCG, trading, and service businesses in Bangladesh. Key Odoo modules for Bangladeshi manufacturers include Manufacturing (MRP), Inventory, Purchase, Sales, Accounting, and HR. Multi-currency support makes it ideal for export-oriented businesses.

What is the difference between Odoo Community and Odoo Enterprise?

+

Odoo Community is free and open-source, covering core business modules. Odoo Enterprise is the paid version with advanced features: mobile apps, IoT integration, accounting localization, and direct Odoo S.A. support. For Bangladesh manufacturers, Odoo Enterprise typically delivers better ROI through its advanced manufacturing and financial modules. Read our Odoo Inventory Valuation guide for a practical comparison.

How do I choose the right Odoo consultant in Bangladesh?

+

When selecting an Odoo ERP consultant in Bangladesh, evaluate: (1) Proven implementations in your industry, (2) A structured discovery and gap analysis process, (3) A clear UAT methodology, (4) Post-go-live support commitment, and (5) Local client references. Avoid consultants who skip the discovery phase — misaligned implementations are the #1 cause of ERP failure. See our Odoo consulting methodology.
