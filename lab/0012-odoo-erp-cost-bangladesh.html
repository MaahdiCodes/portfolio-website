A proper quote requires a discovery session. Everything before that is an estimate.

The five cost buckets

Every Odoo ERP project in Bangladesh — regardless of size — has the same five cost components. Misunderstanding any one of them leads to budget overruns and post-go-live resentment.

  • Odoo License — Community (free) vs. Enterprise (paid per user/month)
  • Consulting & Implementation — Discovery, configuration, customization, go-live management
  • Data Migration — Extracting, cleaning, and importing your existing business data
  • Training — End-user training, administrator training, and post-go-live hypercare
  • Hosting & Ongoing Support — Server infrastructure, maintenance, and future upgrades
Key insight

Most businesses budget well for Items 1 and 2 but severely underestimate Items 3, 4, and 5. In my experience, data migration and training together account for 30–40% of total project cost — and 80% of go-live delays.

Odoo license cost

Odoo Community is completely free and open-source. No license fee, ever. You pay only for implementation, hosting, and support.

Odoo Enterprise (Custom plan, required for on-premise, Odoo.sh, and Studio) is charged per user per month. In 2026, the pricing is:

Plan (Annual billing) USD/user/month Approx. BDT/user/month
Standard (Odoo Online) $7.25 ~BDT 850
Custom (Odoo.sh / On-premise) $10.90 ~BDT 1,300
One App Free / Community Free BDT 0

For a 15-user Custom plan deployment, the annual license cost alone is ~BDT 2.3 lakh. This lower pricing makes the Community vs. Enterprise decision much easier for Bangladesh SMEs compared to previous years.

Decision

Start with Community if you can live without: advanced manufacturing (MRP v2), Odoo Studio, mobile offline mode, and direct Odoo S.A. support. Most Bangladesh SMEs in trading and services can. Most manufacturers cannot — the MRP v2 module alone justifies Enterprise for multi-level BoM operations.

Consulting & implementation cost

This is the most variable and most misunderstood component. A scoped implementation project by an experienced Odoo ERP consultant in Bangladesh is priced by scope — number of modules, level of customization, and complexity of data migration — not by the hour.

Hourly rates for qualified Odoo consultants in Bangladesh currently range from BDT 1,500–4,000/hour for local practitioners to USD 50–120/hour for internationally-positioned firms. The total project fee depends on estimated hours, not the rate card alone.

Any consultant who gives you a fixed price without a discovery session is guessing. And you'll pay for that guess at go-live.

What drives cost up

Understanding cost drivers is more useful than any price table. These are the factors — ranked by impact — that consistently inflate Odoo implementation budgets in Bangladesh:

  • Customization scope Every "Odoo doesn't work the way we do it" becomes a development ticket. In Phase 1, adapt your process to Odoo. Customize only what has no standard equivalent. High impact
  • Data quality Messy master data (items, vendors, customers, BoMs) scattered across spreadsheets adds 30–50% to migration costs. Clean before you migrate. See my Excel → Odoo migration checklist. High impact
  • Scope creep Adding modules mid-project is the most common budget killer. Lock scope in writing at kickoff. Phase 2 is a real thing. Medium impact
  • User count More users = more training = more Enterprise licenses. Audit who actually needs system access vs. who just needs reports. Medium impact
  • Change management Underinvesting in adoption training is the #1 cause of ERP failure. Budget for floor walks, refresher sessions, and named champions. Read my notes on ERP change management. Medium impact
  • Integration requirements Connecting Odoo to legacy systems (e-commerce, barcode hardware, government tax portals) adds development cost. Validate integration scope before signing any contract. Lower impact

Frequently asked questions

Is Odoo Community free in Bangladesh?

Yes — Odoo Community is fully free and open-source. You pay for implementation, hosting, and support, but not the software itself. The trade-off is losing access to Enterprise-only modules: advanced MRP, Odoo Studio, mobile offline, and Odoo S.A. direct support.

What is the cheapest way to implement Odoo ERP in Bangladesh?

Start with Odoo Community, limit Phase 1 to 3 core modules, resist customization pressure, and engage a local Odoo consultant rather than an international firm. A disciplined SME rollout can go live for under BDT 3 lakh — if data is clean and scope is locked.

How long does Odoo ERP implementation take in Bangladesh?

SME projects (1 site, up to 15 users, 3–5 modules) typically take 6–10 weeks from kick-off to go-live. Multi-site enterprise implementations — like a national manufacturing rollout — take 6–18 months, depending on the number of sites, customization depth, and change management investment.
