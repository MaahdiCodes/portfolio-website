Optimizing Manufacturing ERP in Bangladesh

As a specialized manufacturing consultant in Bangladesh, I have led the largest Odoo ERP deployments across spinning, weaving, dyeing, and finishing facilities. Implementing an ERP in a manufacturing plant is vastly different from a retail environment. It requires deep knowledge of shop-floor realities, Bill of Materials (BoM), routing, and precise inventory valuation like AVCO.

Our Approach for RMG and Textiles

Bangladesh's export backbone requires systems that handle multi-currency, end-to-end traceability, and high-volume production planning. Our consulting process includes:

  • Detailed Business Process Modeling Notation (BPMN) for manufacturing workflows.
  • Optimizing Work Centers and Routings to reduce bottlenecks.
  • Integrating Quality Control (QC) directly into the manufacturing orders.
  • Training operators on the floor—ensuring high adoption rates.

Case Study: National Textile Group

Recently, we deployed Odoo across 5 production facilities with over 200 users. By migrating them from fragmented spreadsheets to a unified ERP, we achieved a 60% efficiency gain in production planning and inventory reconciliation. This is what sets us apart as a top Odoo consultant in Bangladesh.
