Odoo partner or independent consultant?

Many people searching for an "Odoo partner in Bangladesh" are really looking for someone who knows Odoo well and will make it work for their business. Those aren't always the same thing. An official Odoo partner is a company in Odoo S.A.'s partner programme: it resells Enterprise licences and usually runs a team of consultants and developers. I'm not an Odoo partner. I'm an independent, Odoo Certified Functional Consultant, and that changes what I can do for you:

  • No licence to sell. I recommend Community or Enterprise, and the modules you need, based on your business, not on a sales target.
  • A second opinion on partner proposals. I review scope, price and timeline in a partner's quote before you sign. See the Odoo quote checker and how to choose an Odoo partner.
  • The client's side of the table. I can run discovery, write the requirements, and oversee the partner's delivery and UAT on your behalf.
  • Rescue when it goes wrong. If an implementation has stalled or the team won't use it, I audit and fix it, whoever built it. See Odoo rescue.

If you need licences, hosting or a large development team, an official partner is the right choice, and I'm happy to work alongside one.

How I work

Implementing an ERP isn't just installing software. It changes how your business operates. My method is the same at every size:

  • Discovery & gap analysis: map how goods, money and approvals actually move before touching configuration (how discovery works).
  • Solution design: standard Odoo flows first, customisation only where it pays for itself.
  • Data migration: clean master data and reconciled opening balances, signed off before go-live.
  • UAT: real scenarios run by your own team, with written sign-off (free UAT & go-live course).
  • Adoption: role-based training, super-users per department, and hypercare after go-live.
